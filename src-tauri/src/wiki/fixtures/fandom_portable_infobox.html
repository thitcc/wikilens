Copper Ore is a type of ore found abundantly in the Undergrounds. It can also be found in smaller quantities in the Clay Caves and Forgotten Ruins.

It is the most common and basic type of ore, and is required to craft Copper Bars which are used for upgraded versions of tools, armor, and crafting stations.
