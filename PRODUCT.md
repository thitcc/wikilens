# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

desktop (Windows)

## Users

PC gamers mid-session. They are playing a wiki-heavy game (Stardew Valley, Conan
Exiles, Core Keeper, …) in borderless/windowed mode, hands on the game, when a
question comes up. They summon the panel with a hotkey, over the running game;
the job to be done is getting one fact and getting back to playing. They are not
"using an app" — they are playing, and WikiLens borrows a few seconds of that.

## Product Purpose

WikiLens answers in-game questions from the game's own MediaWiki — and only from
it — so the player never has to alt-tab or open the wiki at all. Success is a
glance: seconds from hotkey to a trusted fact to back-in-game, with source links
standing by for the moments the player doubts the answer. A later-version
direction (not current scope) is an optional companion mode for longer
back-and-forth — e.g. working out an optimal achievement path in a game like
Guild Wars 2 — but the product today is tuned for the fast glance.

## Positioning

Kills the alt-tab: the fastest path from an in-game question to a trusted answer
while the game keeps running.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, instant. The panel recedes into the game; zero ceremony —
answers appear and get out of the way. Utility is the character: terse factual
voice, no mascots, no flourish. Anything that makes the player wait or look at
WikiLens instead of the game is out of character.

## Anti-references

- Overwolf-style gamer overlay bloat: neon RGB, widget clusters, ads,
  aggressive branding competing with the game for attention.
- Generic AI chatbot styling: chat bubbles, bot avatars, sparkle-emoji "AI"
  decoration, typing indicators.

## Design Principles

- **The game is the main character.** The overlay borrows the screen; it never
  competes with the game for attention. Quiet by default, invisible when
  dismissed.
- **Speed is the interface.** Every interaction is judged by
  time-from-question-to-back-in-game. Ceremony, choreography, and waiting are
  defects.
- **Show the receipts.** Trust comes from grounding, not confidence: every
  answer points at the wiki pages it came from.
- **Familiar over clever.** Standard affordances, quiet chips, owned menus that
  behave like native ones. The tool should disappear into the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readability is the bar: text must stay readable over varied game backdrops
(the near-opaque dark glass exists for this). No formal WCAG target or other
accommodations are committed.
